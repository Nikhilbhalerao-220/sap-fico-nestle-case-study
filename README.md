# SAP FICO Case Study: Nestlé GLOBE Programme

A case study on how Nestlé used SAP to standardise its finance and business processes worldwide through the **GLOBE (Global Business Excellence)** programme.

This is my **Week 3 task** on real-world applications of SAP FICO (Financial Accounting and Controlling).

## What the case study covers

1. **Executive summary**
2. **Company background**: Nestlé's decentralised structure and growth by acquisition
3. **Why SAP finance was implemented**: about 14 separate SAP systems, no single view of the numbers, duplicate purchasing
4. **Implementation process**: governance, FI/CO scope, rollout timeline, change management
5. **Challenges faced**: cost and schedule overruns, local resistance, data standardisation
6. **Benefits realised**: financial visibility, procurement savings, consistent processes, scalability
7. **Lessons learned**
8. **Conclusion**
9. **Sources and limitations**

## Key facts

- **Company:** Nestlé S.A. (Switzerland), food and beverage
- **Programme:** GLOBE, launched in 2000
- **Software:** SAP (mySAP.com / R/3 based)
- **Scope:** finance, procurement, sales, supply chain and HR across about 45 markets
- **Estimated cost:** up to SFr 3 billion

## Note on sources

The case study is based on publicly available material (Swissinfo, IMD, Nestlé Malaysia annual report, Food Business News and academic course material). Module-level FICO details are not public, so they are inferred from GLOBE's stated goals and are labelled as such in the document. Some figures come from secondary sources and may vary between publications.
